ShazamX 0.4.0 restores song recognition for Shazam 5.5.1 on iOS 6 and Shazam 7.2.0 on iOS 7 (8-10 may work, needs testing). Install the matching Shazam app first.

Netrun is the default recognition server. Render remains available in Settings > ShazamX.

On Shazam 7.2.0, Add to Tags opens My Tags. Saved tags show their cached cover art.
