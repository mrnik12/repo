ShazamX 0.3.8 restores song recognition for Shazam 5.5.1 on iOS 6 and Shazam 7.2.0 on iOS 7. Install the matching Shazam app first.

Netrun is the default recognition server. Render remains available in Settings > ShazamX.

Shows Listening and Sending inline and supports recognition from the bottom Shazam tab. Shazam 5.5.1 uses frequency-reactive ring movement. Shazam 7.2.0 returns to its flat idle ring after recognition.

Known limitation: Opening a saved tag can remain on the loading screen.
