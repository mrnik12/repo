ShazamX 0.3.8 restores song recognition for Shazam 5.5.1 on iOS 6 and Shazam 7.2.0 on iOS 7 (8-10 may work, needs testing). Install the matching Shazam app first.

Netrun is the default recognition server. Render remains available in Settings > ShazamX.

Known limitation: Opening a saved tag can remain on the loading screen on Shazam 7.2.0.
